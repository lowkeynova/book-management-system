    How Smart Stock Advisor Works

    The advisor combines three things you already track — current inventory level, minimum stock level and sales history — and applies simple rules to suggest what to reorder. Books that sell fast but sit near their minimum level are flagged first. The health score (0–100) blends stock cover against the minimum level with how quickly the book has been selling in the last 30 days. This is a rule-based inventory recommendation feature, not machine learning or AI.
